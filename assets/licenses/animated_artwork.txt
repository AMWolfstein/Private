Animated video artwork (Apple Music motion covers)
Adapted for Sistrum from Mornye and Mornye-Extension (https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye, https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye-Extension).
Copyright 2026 zarzet and contributors. LGPL-3.0-only.

The single-file HLS rules (playlist variant choice, byte-range fragments of one
MP4, and the complete-download check) follow Mornye's motion artwork download
code. The Apple Music catalog lookup (developer token strategies, tall/motion
key priority, retry and expiry handling) follows Mornye-Extension's Apple Music
source. The Dart code in lib/services/animated_artwork/ is rewritten for
Sistrum's architecture and is not an extension or plugin host.

This feature uses an unofficial Apple Music web interface. It is not an
official or stable API, and Apple may change or block it at any time.

The complete LGPL-3.0 terms are in lgpl-3.0.txt in this folder.
